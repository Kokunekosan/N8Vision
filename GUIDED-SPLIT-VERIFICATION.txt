boxes in the parent crop. Mouse/touch drawing, Undo hint, optional 0-100 YOLAST
pass budget (default17). Boxes prompt SAM, never clip its output to the hints.

with parent foreground, then optional bounded YOLAST naming. Existing masks,
labels and teaching data stay unchanged while the background job runs.

checks prevent applying stale results. Job results contain only review metadata;
mask drafts fetched separately. Review last completed split supports reopening.
