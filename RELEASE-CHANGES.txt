# Official alpha — I.2t-l-Z1a

This complete release consolidates the work since I.2t-l:

- Mobile browsing with bounded loading, orientation-specific column counts, foreground filter cards and Clear Selected.
- One shared environment with Lite, Blurred and Full launchers/install capacity.
- Selected-image subject assembly with optional PartGLEE/LangHOPS and the configured image-capable LM through ComfyUI.
- Explicit manual mask ownership, confirmed composite lessons, real class-head training with replay, validation, rollback and one coalesced follow-up queue.
- Training lights/status and per-class validation outcomes; adding lessons while training no longer invalidates unchanged candidate inputs.
- Merged feature badges/filters, retained detector attribution, overlapping-frame suppression and intentional removed-detector cleanup.
- Inspector/mask-studio zoom, Fit, WASD and touch-stick framing.

Download the GitHub-Ready ZIP for a clean complete install. Existing users: stop
Vision, replace source while preserving your environment, archive, caches, models,
settings and training data, restart and refresh browsers. The source-only update
ZIP targets the previous public I.2t-l release.

Subject assembly/LM interrogation are specialist selected-image operations.
Automatic class training consumes confirmed lessons only. Class training updates
a visual class head; it does not fine-tune the preferred LM or ownership parser.
Validation rejection retains the previous model. Three sources/class is a minimum
split gate, not a generalization claim. Optional LangHOPS retains noncommercial
upstream terms. Downloaded models/dependencies are not part of this MIT source ZIP.

Controlled source/API/training and archive checks passed; broader phone, fresh
Internet-install and GPU/Comfy compatibility testing remains ongoing. Recent mobile
and framing controls have simulated checks; report real-device issues in GitHub.

This is the official alpha baseline. Through Friday, October 16, 2026, changes are
limited to polishing, fixes, compatibility and documentation.
