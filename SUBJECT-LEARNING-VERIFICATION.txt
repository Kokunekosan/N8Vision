T1 — manual subject teaching capture

Apply over Q1. Stop Vision, extract patch, restart and refresh the browser.
No dependency install or new model download required.

The inspector has Learn Subject. It is disabled for untouched automatic subjects,
ordinary detections, same-label corrections and already committed lessons.
Manually changing a Subject noun marks the resulting subject ready to teach.
Select that subject after relabeling, then press Learn Subject to commit it.
The eligibility survives closing the inspector and restarting Vision.

To teach ownership, select an ordinary part MASK and press Add part to subject.
Choose the owning masked Subject from the numbered list (boxes distinguish equal
names). The subject retains the original part geometry as a manually confirmed
attachment and becomes ready to teach. A part already attached cannot be added
again or attached to another subject through this action. Another whole Subject
cannot be attached as a part. Paint a mask first if the part is only a detector box.
This is manual ownership capture, not automatic anatomical reconstruction.

Learn Subject saves an immutable lesson snapshot into the archive database:
subject label, original label/change reason, existing mask, manually attributed
part masks, source path, SHA256 content identity and source stat signature.
Commit is exactly once per manual revision; stale selections are rejected.
The button disables after commitment. Masks and ordinary detections are preserved.
No automatic YOLO/LM result creates a training lesson. A name change alone does
not assert that every anatomical relationship was manually verified.
