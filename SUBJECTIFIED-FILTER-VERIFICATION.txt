Z1d: Subjectified archive-status filter.
Search Subjectified in Filters; add its magnet. + shows images with named
subjects; - excludes those images and keeps unfinished/unprocessed ones.
Requires at least one positive subject observation and every active subject
named; unknown/unidentified labels, unresolved splits or overlapping split
masks remain unfinished. Ordinary detections do not count as subjectification.
This is completion of labeling, not proof of label accuracy or manual training.
No synthetic observation/badge is written. State follows current observations.
Filter works with presets. Subject separation also applies this active filter
to selected images, including hidden selections retained from earlier views.
No automatic inference starts when a filter changes. No model/dependency changes.
Verified JS syntax, seven representative subject states, +/- filtering,
batch exclusion and stable filter identity for preset roundtrips.

patch, restart and refresh browser. Exact ZIP reconstruction/CRC/hash verified.
